## About us

**LARION** is a Vietnam-based AI &amp; IT outsourcing company with **20+ years** of experience delivering enterprise software to clients around the world. This GitHub account is our **Technology &amp; Innovation** arm — the place where our engineers open-source the emerging technology tools, references, and experiments that come out of our R&amp;D activities, so the wider developer community can benefit from them too.

We build across the full software lifecycle: AI development, custom enterprise software, mobile apps, QA &amp; testing, DevOps, data &amp; analytics, and core banking modernization — for industries spanning banking &amp; fintech, healthcare, education, e-commerce, and real estate.
